Privacy Policy for Dwella

Last Updated: September 1, 2026

Introduction

Welcome to Dwella ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your data when you use the Dwella mobile application (the "App").

By using Dwella, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Information We Collect

1. Account Information

When you create an account or sign in to Dwella, we collect:

  • Name: Your display name for identification within the app
  • Email Address: Used for account authentication and communication
  • Profile Picture: Optional image you upload to personalize your profile
  • Authentication Data: Your password is handled by Firebase Authentication and is never visible to us

2. Task and Household Data

To provide the core functionality of the App, we collect and store:

  • Tasks: Task names, descriptions, due dates, due times, completion status, repeat settings, assigned rooms, and assigned household members
  • Household Information: Household names, invite codes, member lists, and household creation dates
  • Completion History: Records of when tasks are marked as complete
  • Room Data: Names of rooms you create for organizing tasks
  • Grocery Lists: Item names, amounts, aisle categories, and whether an item has been ticked off
  • Recipes: Titles, ingredients, method steps, servings, timings, and any source link you import from
  • Meal Plans: Which recipes you have planned, on which days, for which meal, and for how many people

Photographs of recipes are read on your device. When you add a recipe by photographing a page, the text is recognised locally on your iPhone and only the resulting recipe is saved. The photograph itself is never uploaded to us or to anyone else.

3. Usage Data

We may collect information about how you interact with the App, including:

  • Device Information: Device type, operating system version, and app version
  • Feature Usage: Which parts of the App are used and where people stop during setup, recorded against your household rather than against you personally, and describing the shape of an action rather than its content — see PostHog under Third-Party Services below
  • Crash Reports: Technical information about where in our code a failure occurred, so we can fix it. Crash reports do not contain your content

4. Location Data

We do NOT collect or track your location data.

How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  1. To Provide Core Services:
    • Create and manage your user account
    • Enable task creation, assignment, and tracking
    • Facilitate household collaboration and member management
    • Sync your data across devices
  2. To Improve the App:
    • Analyze app performance and fix bugs
    • Develop new features and improvements
    • Ensure app security and prevent abuse
  3. To Communicate With You:
    • Send important updates about the App
    • Respond to your support requests
    • Notify you about household invitations (in-app only)
  4. To Enable Collaboration:
    • Share task and household data with members of your household
    • Display profile information to other household members

How We Store and Protect Your Data

Data Storage

  • Cloud Storage: Your data is stored using Google Firebase Firestore, a secure cloud-based database service
  • Local Storage: Some data is cached locally on your device using SwiftData for offline access and improved performance
  • Profile Pictures: Stored as compressed base64-encoded data in Firestore (limited to approximately 800KB per image)

Security Measures

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data:

  • Data is transmitted over secure, encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Firebase Authentication provides secure user authentication
  • Access to your data is restricted to authorized household members only
  • We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes

Data Retention

  • Your data is retained as long as your account is active
  • You can delete your tasks and household data at any time through the App
  • When you delete your account, your personal data will be removed from our systems

Third-Party Services

Dwella uses the following third-party services that may collect information:

Google Firebase

We use Firebase for:

  • Firebase Authentication: Secure user authentication and account management
  • Cloud Firestore: Cloud-based database for storing and syncing your data
  • Firebase Cloud Functions: Server-side processing for household management

Firebase's data practices are governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

RevenueCat

We use RevenueCat to manage household subscriptions. RevenueCat receives your household identifier, your user identifier, and your purchase and subscription status. It does not receive your name, email address, or any of your household's content. RevenueCat's data practices are governed by RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

PostHog (Analytics and Crash Reporting)

We use PostHog, hosted in the United States, to understand how Dwella is used and to be told when the app crashes. This helps us find problems and decide what to improve.

Analytics events are recorded against your household identifier rather than against you personally. What we send is limited to the shape of an action, not its content. For example:

  • That a task was created, whether it repeats, and whether it was assigned — never the task's name or who it was assigned to
  • That a grocery item was added and which aisle category it fell into — never the item itself
  • That a recipe was saved, how it was added, and how many ingredients and steps it had — never the recipe's title or contents
  • Which screens in the app were reached, and where people stop during setup

We never send your name, other household members' names, your household's name, email addresses, invite codes, recipe titles, task names, grocery item names, or any note you write in the app.

If Dwella crashes, a crash report is sent so we can fix it. A crash report contains technical information about where in our code the failure happened. It does not contain your content.

We have disabled PostHog's location lookup, so no city, region, or approximate location is recorded. PostHog's data practices are governed by PostHog's Privacy Policy.

Data Sharing and Disclosure

Within Your Household

When you join or create a household in Dwella:

  • Your name and profile picture are visible to other household members
  • Tasks you create or are assigned to are visible to all household members
  • Your task completion activity is visible to household members

We Do NOT Share Your Data With:

  • Advertisers or marketing companies
  • Data brokers
  • Any third parties for their own marketing purposes

Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required to do so by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities (e.g., court orders, subpoenas).

Your Rights and Choices

You have the following rights regarding your data:

  1. Access: You can view all your data within the App
  2. Correction: You can edit your profile information, tasks, and household data at any time
  3. Deletion: You can delete tasks, leave households, or delete your account
  4. Data Portability: You can request a copy of your data by contacting us
  5. Opt-Out: You can sign out and stop using the App at any time

How to Exercise Your Rights

  • To edit your profile: Go to the More tab → Settings → Edit Profile
  • To delete tasks: Swipe left on any task or use the delete option
  • To leave a household: Go to household settings and select "Leave Household"
  • To delete your account: Contact us at buildwellstudios@gmail.com
